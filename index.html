Every platform you use runs on the same quiet transaction: your attention, traded for a prediction of what you'll do next. CHAPTER ONE

The Feed Learns Your Face

Nobody sat down and decided to build a machine that knows you're sad before you do. It happened one small optimization at a time — a feature added to keep you a few seconds longer, then another, then a third that noticed the first two worked. Nothing in the system had to understand you to predict you. It only had to watch.

Here is what actually gets recorded when you open the app: not just what you tap, but what you almost tapped and didn't. Not just what you watched, but the half-second you spent hovering before you kept scrolling. That hesitation is data too, and it is worth more than the click, because hesitation is where your actual preferences live — the ones you haven't said out loud, sometimes even to yourself.
